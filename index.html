The science behind the story

Ghost Mail models BB84 key distribution and intercept-resend attacks. Ghost Relay models teleportation with a shared entangled pair, two classical outcome bits, and conditional corrections.

These are classical simulations. Game points are teaching rules, and simulator replays reveal information that real participants do not have. Read the sources and model limits in each guide: Ghost Mail ↗ · Ghost Relay ↗.
